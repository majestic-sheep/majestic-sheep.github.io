Music

These are the best songs I've made, in no particular order. Click on the song title for a PDF of the score. Some scores may contain chord or structure annotations that I used when writing the song.

Despite how much more music there is here than programs or stories, I'm not a music major! This is just a hobby. It's fun, but I'm a programmer before I'm a composer.

Mixed Instrument Songs

Eventually, I started experimenting with other MuseScore 4 instruments. I made songs for real instruments as well as songs for synthesizers.

My favorite song here is Yearn for the Tavern, which I wrote at Carnegie Mellon University's National High School Game Academy summer program. It was inspired by the Dwarf Fortress sound track, a game I was obsessed with at the time.
